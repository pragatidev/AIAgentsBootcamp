# TalentFlow versus a recruiting ATS

TalentFlow scores resumes against one job description and drafts emails from templates. The graph is talentflow/graphs/score_resumes.py. This page is keep versus replace. It does not take a product down.

| keep | replace |
| --- | --- |
| the leftover seat: the ATS seat for interview scheduling, offer letters, and compliance | the hand-read screening pile the pipeline already scores |
| recruiter judgement on the shortlist | copy-paste of scores into a spreadsheet |

Leftover seat: the ATS seat. TalentFlow does not delete it. Scheduling, offers, and compliance stay on that seat.

What the pipeline covers: N resumes scored, one summary, template emails.

What it does not: source candidates, schedule interviews, send offers, store identity files, or take the ATS down.
